Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

JayR Social Publisher is designed to help users authorize social media accounts and prepare or upload content using official platform APIs.

Information used

When a user connects a supported social account, the service may process authorization information and account data made available by that platform, as necessary to provide requested publishing features.

Passwords

JayR Social Publisher does not require users to provide their TikTok password. Authentication is handled by TikTok through its authorization system.

Content

Content selected for publishing may be processed to perform the upload or publishing action requested by the user.

Sharing

Information is not sold. Data may be transmitted to connected social platforms when necessary to provide user-requested functionality.

Data choices

Users may revoke a connected platform's authorization through the relevant platform settings. Requests concerning data handled directly by JayR Social Publisher may be sent to jrlewis45@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service develops or its integrations change.
