JayR Social Publisher

JayR Social Publisher is a creator tool for preparing and sharing original short-form video content to supported social media platforms.

The service is being developed to let users authorize their own social accounts and upload content through official platform APIs.

TikTok integration

Users authenticate with TikTok directly. JayR Social Publisher does not ask users to provide their TikTok passwords.
